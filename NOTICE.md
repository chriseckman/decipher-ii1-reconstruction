`inputs/source_transcription.txt` contains a transcription of Jonathan Swift's *Gulliver's Travels*, Part III, Chapter 2. The compatible printed witness is Raymond Macdonald Alden's *Readings in English Prose of the Eighteenth Century* (1911 copyright), p. 97. The underlying Swift literary text is treated as public-domain material in the retained source record; no scanned page or later editorial apparatus is distributed.
